Why Choose CloudeWave VPS Hosting?

Our Virtual Private Servers are built from the ground up for developers and businesses that demand uncompromising performance, security, and control.

Blazing Fast NVMe Storage

Traditional SSDs are a thing of the past. Our VPS nodes utilize enterprise-grade NVMe SSD storage arrayed in RAID configurations. This ensures your read/write speeds are incredibly fast, reducing latency for database queries, website loading times, and intensive data processing tasks by up to 10x compared to standard SATA drives.

AMD Ryzen™ 9 Processing Power

Compute performance matters. That's why we power our infrastructure with AMD Ryzen™ 9 7950X and EPYC processors. With high single-thread clock speeds, your applications and heavy workloads will execute faster than ever. Say goodbye to CPU bottlenecks and unpredictable shared hosting performance.

Enterprise DDoS Protection

Security isn't an add-on; it's a foundation. Every VPS comes standard with always-on, enterprise-level DDoS mitigation. Our automated systems detect and filter malicious traffic within seconds, ensuring your server remains online and accessible even during massive volumetric attacks up to several Terabits per second.

Full Root Access & Control

Your server, your rules. You get absolute administrative control over your VPS. Install any software, modify kernel parameters, configure custom firewalls, or set up Docker containers. We provide the raw infrastructure; you bring the vision. Access your server securely via SSH anytime, anywhere.

Instant Global Deployment

Time is money. Once your payment clears, our automated provisioning system deploys your server in under 60 seconds. Choose from our strategically located data centers to host your applications as close to your user base as possible, ensuring minimal latency and optimal routing.

Dedicated Resources

We do not oversell. Period. The RAM, CPU cores, and storage allocated to your plan are yours to use 24/7. This guarantees stable, predictable performance without the "noisy neighbor" effect common in cheaper, overcrowded hosting environments. You get exactly what you pay for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our VPS hosting services.

01 What Operating Systems are supported?

We support a wide variety of Linux distributions including Ubuntu (20.04, 22.04), Debian, AlmaLinux, Rocky Linux, and CentOS. You can easily select your preferred OS during the checkout process and it will be installed automatically.

02 Can I upgrade my VPS later?

Absolutely. Our infrastructure is designed to scale with your needs. You can upgrade your CPU, RAM, and Storage at any time seamlessly through our client portal without losing your data.

03 Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, we offer a 24-hour refund policy on our VPS plans. If you are not completely satisfied with the performance or service, simply open a ticket within the first 24 hours of your purchase for a full refund.

04 Are these servers managed or unmanaged?

Our VPS plans are strictly unmanaged. We provide the underlying infrastructure, power, network connectivity, and hardware support. You are responsible for configuring and managing the software inside your server.
