Why Host Your Discord Bot With CloudeWave?

We built our platform specifically to solve the headaches of bot hosting. From complex dependencies to uptime monitoring, we handle the infrastructure so you can focus on coding.

Multi-Language Support

Whether you write in Discord.js (Node.js), Discord.py (Python), JDA (Java), or the blazing-fast Bun runtime, our platform natively supports your code. We maintain up-to-date environments with pre-installed package managers like npm, pip, and maven so your bot runs flawlessly out of the box.

24/7 Guaranteed Uptime

A bot that goes offline loses trust. Our infrastructure is designed for 99.9% uptime. If a hardware node fails, our automated systems instantly migrate and restart your container to ensure your bot remains online in your Discord servers without interruption.

Enterprise DDoS Protection

Discord bots are frequent targets of denial-of-service attacks. We automatically filter malicious traffic at the network edge using enterprise-grade DDoS mitigation capable of absorbing multi-terabit attacks, completely invisible to your bot's operation.

Custom Control Panel

Manage your bot through our intuitive, custom-built control panel. View live console logs in real-time, upload files via an integrated web-based file manager, manage databases, and restart your instances with a single click. No complex SSH commands required.

Instant Deployment

Don't wait hours for manual provisioning. Once you select a plan and check out, your hosting environment is created in seconds. Simply upload your code, input your bot token in the environment variables, and click start.

Automated Backups

Accidentally deleted your config file or corrupted your SQLite database? We perform automated daily backups of all your bot files. You can restore your entire instance to a previous state directly from your control panel with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about hosting your Discord bot with us.

01 Do I need to keep my computer on?

No! Once you upload your bot code to our servers and click start, our infrastructure takes over. Your bot will remain online 24/7 even if you turn off your personal computer.

02 How do I upload my bot's code?

You can upload your files via our drag-and-drop web file manager in the control panel, connect directly using SFTP with any client like FileZilla, or pull directly from a GitHub repository.

03 Will my free bot "fall asleep"?

Unlike other free hosting platforms that put your app to sleep when no one is using it, our free Developer tier keeps your Discord bot awake and online 24/7 without needing external ping services.

04 Can I host a music bot?

Yes! We have specialized JVM-optimized servers specifically for Lavalink and music bots. We recommend checking out our dedicated Lavalink hosting plans for the best audio latency and performance.
